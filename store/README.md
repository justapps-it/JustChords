Just Chords is the app for guitar (and ukulele) players: find the chords, learn to play them and take them on stage.

🎚 TUNER, METRONOME AND STAGE
Accurate tuner, metronome, live chord recognition, setlists and a stage mode with autoscroll and dark theme.

Everything works without an account. With Google or email you sync songs, setlists and progress across your devices. Paste a playlist and get song suggestions for your level.

### Data safety

Data is **encrypted in transit** (HTTPS). Users **can request deletion** (in the app, on the website, or at https://chords.justapps.it/delete-account). No data is shared with third parties. Cloudflare runs our server as a service provider, which does not count as sharing.

| Data type | Collected | Optional | Ephemeral | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Personal info → Name | Yes | Yes (account) | No | Account management, App functionality |
| Personal info → Email address | Yes | Yes (account) | No | Account management, App functionality |
| Personal info → User IDs | Yes | Yes (account) | No | Account management |
| Audio → Voice or sound recordings | Yes | Yes ("Synced lyrics") | **Yes**: transcribed and discarded | App functionality |
| App activity → Other actions (taste profile, lessons done) | Yes | Yes (account) | No | App functionality, Personalization |
| App activity → Other user-generated content (synced library, setlists) | Yes | Yes (account) | No | App functionality |

Not collected: location, contacts, photos, files, health, financial info, messages, calendar, device IDs, crash logs, analytics.
Microphone audio for the tuner and the chord checks stays on the device. So does the audio of analysed videos, except the optional lyrics clips listed above.

### Sensitive permissions

- **Foreground service type `mediaProjection`** (Policy → App content → Foreground service permissions): used for "Analyse while it plays". It captures the audio of a video playing inside the app, only after the user accepts Android's screen-recording prompt, to recognise its chords. Google asks for a short video of the feature: record "Analizza video → Analizza mentre suona".
- **RECORD_AUDIO:** tuner, chord recognition, lesson checks, level test.
- **POST_NOTIFICATIONS:** the notification shown while capturing.

## Release

1. **Play App Signing:** for the first upload, Google can generate the app signing key, and the key of the AAB (SHA-1 `55:8C:2F:…:B6:74`) becomes the upload key. Users who installed the APK from GitHub then have to reinstall from Play once, because the signatures differ.
2. **Testing:** start with **Internal testing** (instant) to check it on your phone.
3. **Production:** create the release, upload the AAB, release notes from the GitHub release, then send it for review.

### Policy risks to know

- **Downloading audio/video from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram** ("Analyse from the link", the native YouTube player, "Play with the original") is contrary to YouTube's terms. Google Play usually rejects apps that do this, under the "Unauthorized use of copyrighted content" / "Abuse of third-party services" policies. If the app is rejected, the fix is a Play version without those features (playback capture, microphone and files keep working).
- **Chords and lyrics** come from third-party sites (Ultimate Guitar, Accordi e Spartiti), fetched by the user's device. Rights holders can send takedown notices.
